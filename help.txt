    OriSim3D — mouse / touch interactions
======================================

Everything happens on the single 3D view. Selected points/segments/faces
highlight; a drag draws a green line from where you pressed down to the
current pointer position (orange instead while moving an already-selected
point).

Points
------
- Click a point: select / deselect it (toggle).
- Drag from a point to a different point:
    - no crease connects them yet -> adds a crease directly between them (by3d)
    - a crease already connects them -> adds the perpendicular-bisector
      crease instead (across3d)
- Drag from a point to a segment (or a segment to a point): adds a crease
  from the segment, perpendicular to it, through the point (p3d).
- Drag an already-selected point: moves it in 3D, following the pointer
  (`move` then `check` to reconcile lengths); the drag line turns orange.
  A short tap (<4px of movement) on an already-selected point just
  deselects it instead of moving it.

Segments
--------
- Click a segment: select / deselect it (toggle); also logs its 2D vs 3D
  length in the panel.
- Drag from a segment to a different segment: adds the bisector crease
  between them (bisector3d).
- Drag from a point while a segment is already selected: shows a live
  rotation-angle label (rounded to the nearest 10°) as you move the
  pointer. Releasing rotates every currently-selected point around that
  segment by the shown angle.

Faces
-----
- Click a face: select / deselect it. Faces can be stacked (several
  layers at the same screen position) — clicking selects the *whole*
  stack together, and logs each one's index and offset.
- Drag from a face to a different face: adds a crease along the drag
  line, splitting whatever segments it crosses (used to crease across
  several faces in one motion).

Camera
------
- Drag on empty space (nothing under the pointer), left button: rotates
  the camera around the model.
- Drag on empty space, right button: pans (translates) the camera.
- Mouse wheel / pinch: zooms in and out (clamped between 0.2x and 3x).
- Double-click / double-tap on empty space: resets the camera (rotation,
  pan, zoom) and fits the model back into view.

Other
-----
- Ctrl/Cmd+Z: undo the last command (ignored while typing in a text
  field, e.g. the command input, so native text-undo works there
  instead).
